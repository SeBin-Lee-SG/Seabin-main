\section{Professional Summary}
\vspace{-2pt}
Robotics researcher specializing in large-scale \textbf{Multi-Robot Task Allocation (MRTA)} for \textbf{Multi-Robot Systems}, spanning both multi-mobile-robot and multi-manipulator platforms. Also working on \textbf{Multi-Agent Path Finding (MAPF)} and \textbf{Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)} for scalable coordination and supervision, and on building simulation environments that combine real-time interaction, visualization, and robot behavior modeling.
